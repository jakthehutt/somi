# Design context for impeccable

This file pre-answers the Context Gathering Protocol in the impeccable skill. Do not run `/impeccable teach` — the context is already captured here and, in depth, in [tasks/brand.md](tasks/brand.md).

## Design Context

### Users

- **Primary — the owner.** An adult who has decided certain websites are bad for them and wants a structural commitment device, not a willpower app. Uses the dashboard in two modes: **planning mode** (mornings, calm, adding blocks) and **temptation mode** (late at night, tired, wanting to unlock). The UI must serve both, especially the second.
- **Secondary — the friend.** A trusted human chosen by the owner to approve or deny unlock requests. Uses the product briefly and infrequently. Needs context to make a fair call.

Both users are on the same side — no adversarial framing.

### Brand personality

**Three words, in order of weight:** resolute, calm, humane.

- **Resolute** — the product keeps its word. No backdoors, no emergency unlock. Copy is direct; type is firm.
- **Calm** — no flashing red, no scolding, no dark patterns. Under temptation the UI should feel like a reading lamp, not an alarm.
- **Humane** — a written promise between two people. Small, personal, not SaaS.

**Feels like:** a handwritten pact on good paper · a monastery rule book · the Light Phone · Muji stationery.

**Does not feel like:** a productivity dashboard with gradient cards · a crypto wallet with cyan-on-navy · a habit tracker with streak flames · a corporate SaaS with illustrations and "Oops!" empty states.

### Aesthetic direction

**Considered paper.** Warm ivory surface, inked charcoal text, a single oxblood accent reserved for the lock itself and primary action. Left-aligned. Serifed headings that feel set, not rendered. Generous breathing room. **Light theme only** — dark mode is deliberately not supported.

**Type pairing (both on Google Fonts, neither on impeccable's reflex-reject list):**
- Display — **Young Serif** (wedged, stamp-like, 400 only).
- Body / UI — **Literata** (variable, designed for Books, warm).
- Mono — **JetBrains Mono** (only for domains, timestamps, code).

**Color — OKLCH, tinted toward the brand hue (~70°), never pure white or pure black.** Full palette and tokens in [tasks/brand.md §6.2](tasks/brand.md).

**Anti-references (explicit bans on top of impeccable's):**
- Generic Tailwind gray (`gray-50`, `gray-900`) — neutral toward blue, clashes with the paper tint.
- Red / yellow / green Tailwind status palettes — use oxblood / amber / sage only.
- Hero metrics, feature-tile grids, icon-above-heading stacks.
- Exclamation marks and emoji in copy.

### Design principles

1. **Light theme, paper-toned.** Every neutral tints toward warm (~70° hue). Never default to `bg-white` / `bg-gray-50`.
2. **Type is the identity.** Young Serif for display, Literata for everything else. No Inter, no DM Sans, no Fraunces. If you'd reflexively pick a sans for "technical", you are wrong — this product is type-led and serif-first.
3. **One accent, used sparingly.** Oxblood is the lock, sage is "released / approved", amber is "pending". Together they should cover ~10% of any view's visual weight.
4. **Hairlines over cards.** Prefer 1px rules and padding over rounded-xl shadowed boxes. If you find yourself writing a card inside a card, delete one.
5. **Copy is a neutral witness, not a coach.** No streaks, no badges, no "stay strong", no emoji. Specific times beat vague ones ("14:02 tomorrow" beats "in 24 hours").

### Accessibility

- WCAG AA is the minimum bar: contrast, focus indicators, keyboard navigation, labeled inputs.
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44 px on mobile.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` — drop transitions to 0ms.
- No color-only signaling: status badges carry text, not just color.

### Quality bar

**Flagship**, not MVP. This is a small personal product that should feel considered in every detail. Polish budget is generous; spend it.
